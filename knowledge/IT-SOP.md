# IT incident and access requests

For device failures, record the asset number, operating system, exact symptom, first occurrence time, and whether a restart was attempted. Do not repeatedly restart a device that shows burning smells, liquid damage, or repeated blue screens; disconnect power and escalate.

For account, software, VPN, or permission requests, record the requested system, business reason, manager approval if required, and the required completion time.

Security incidents such as phishing, malware, or suspicious login prompts must use high priority and be escalated to the security owner.
